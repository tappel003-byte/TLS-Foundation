Someone already did the hard-looking part. Piers along the wall, brackets painted, grade patched. Summer comes back around and the door binds anyway.

That is not a mystery if the cooler bleed, the condensate line, the downspout, or the leak is still feeding the same clay. The shafts can be in competent ground and the soil around the house can still swell and shrink on a schedule. Piers transfer load. They do not shut off a hose.

The second visit is unglamorous. Follow the water. If it is still landing on the repaired side, the first job treated the symptom and left the engine running. Extending the downspout after the fact is not an insult to the piers. It is the half of the work that never got done.

If the water is actually off and the line is still moving, then you are in the other note — depth, the wrong mechanism, two soil profiles. Check the water first. It is the cheaper question.
