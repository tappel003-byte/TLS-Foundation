Stucco cracks. Brick veneer steps. A hairline at a window is often the finish doing what a finish does on a house that moves a little with heat and dry air. New Mexico finishes take a beating. Not every mark in the coating is a soil problem.

Look past it. Doors. Stem wall. Interior gypsum. Elevations if you have them. If the only distress is in the stucco and the house is quiet everywhere else, you are looking at a coating. A pier bid written from the curb because the brown coat is mapped is a bid written from the wrong layer.

If the stucco crack lines up with a binding door and a slab that's changing, then the finish is just the part you can see. The finish still isn't the diagnosis. It's the clue. Chase the soil and the water. Don't sell steel to a scratch coat.
