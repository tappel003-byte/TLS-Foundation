The garage slab was poured later, or poured separately, or poured on fill that the house never sat on. It has its own joints, its own thickness, and often its own soil. A crack at the laundry door into the garage is not automatically the house failing. It is two structures meeting.

People want one bid for "the slab." The house can be quiet while the garage apron drops. The garage can be fine while the addition next to it is the fill problem. Treating them as one foundation is how you pier the wrong rectangle.

Walk them separately. Survey them separately if you're surveying. If the living room elevations don't match the garage story, believe both readings. They are allowed to disagree. They were never one pour.
